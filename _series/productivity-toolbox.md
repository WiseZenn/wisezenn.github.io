Welcome to the **Productivity Toolbox**.

This series is not a comprehensive tool catalog. It documents the tools that actually make it into daily workflows: how to set them up, how to use them, where their limits are, and how they fit alongside AI agents.

Current topics include:

- **OpenCLI**: turn websites and logged-in browser sessions into deterministic CLI commands for agents;
- **Zotero + LLM**: let models read your library, tags, highlights, and notes to support literature review;
- **Typora + Markdown**: configure a comfortable writing environment and make notes easier to reuse.
